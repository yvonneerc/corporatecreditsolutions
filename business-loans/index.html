Get Business Loans & Credit Lines — Even When the Banks Say "No"

According to the United States Department of Revenue, the majority of business loans and credit lines that are available today don't come from big conventional banks. Most business loans now come from alternative lenders and investors. These alternative lenders typically only offer 1–2 limited funding programs, which can ruin your chances of getting a good loan if you don't meet their specific requirements.

With Corporate Credit Solutions, we work with all legitimate types of funding programs through hundreds of investors and lenders. This gives you the best opportunity to get the most financing — at the best terms.

Right away, you'll be assigned your own personal Finance Officer, who will work directly with you to develop your Finance Blueprint with all the funding options you can qualify for now. Once you choose your funding, our team goes into action, working with lenders and investors to get you approved, negotiating rates and terms, and helping you through all aspects of the funding process until your loan proceeds are in your bank account.

With our help, and when you know how the process works, you can get a business loan or credit line:

  • Regardless of personal credit quality
  • Even with no assets to pledge as collateral
  • As a newer startup business
  • With no financials, or with returns that show a loss
