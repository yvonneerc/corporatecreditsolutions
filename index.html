Our Proven Process

Step-by-step, guided by a dedicated advisor — from foundation to funding.

1

Foundation & Fundability

We make sure your business meets lender and credit issuer credibility standards. Then your information is cross-checked against underwriting guidelines for over 2,100 finance institutions to pre-qualify you for funding programs.

2

Activate Credit Bureaus

We set up your business credit profile with Dun & Bradstreet, Experian Commercial, and Equifax Commercial — including your free D-U-N-S number — and monitor your reports in real time.

3

Build Trade Lines

You get approved for vendor and store credit that reports to the business bureaus, building your profile rapidly so you can move to revolving credit cards and unsecured financing.

4

Unsecured Business Financing

Apply for the full range of programs — unsecured credit lines, revenue financing, equipment leasing, A/R financing, SBA loans, and more — matched to what you qualify for.
