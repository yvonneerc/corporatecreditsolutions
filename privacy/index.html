Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us — including name, email address, phone number, business information, financial information you submit for funding evaluations, and any other information you choose to provide.

Our website host also records standard server log information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Process funding consultations and lender matches
  • Send service updates, transactional emails, and (with your consent) marketing emails
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Sharing of Information

We do NOT sell or rent your personal contact information for any marketing purposes whatsoever.

We may share information with:

  • Lender partners — only with your explicit consent, to facilitate funding applications you request
  • Service providers — companies that help us operate this website and process what you submit through it, under confidentiality obligations. The providers used on this site are our website host (Netlify) and our online form provider (Cognito Forms), which processes applications submitted through our funding application form.
  • Legal compliance — when required by law, court order, or to protect our rights

4. Data Security

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including encryption in transit where appropriate. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, complete requested funding consultations or lender matches, maintain business and transaction records, comply with legal and regulatory obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Retention periods may vary according to the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected. When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize it.

6. Cookies and Third-Party Services

This site does not run our own analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. To remember that you have dismissed a promotional message, we store a single value in your browser's session storage, which your browser clears when you close it.

Some pages embed content provided by third parties. When that content loads, the provider may set its own cookies or receive your IP address and browser information:

  • Google Fonts — web fonts, loaded on all pages
  • Google Maps — the location map on our Contact page
  • Trustpilot — the review rating widget on our home page
  • Cognito Forms — the application form on our Same Day Funding Application page

These services handle information under their own privacy policies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some site features may not function properly.

7. Your Privacy Rights and Consumer Requests

Depending on where you live and which privacy laws apply to you, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Request a copy of your personal information
  • Withdraw consent you previously gave us
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Appeal a decision we make about a privacy request

To make a request, or to appeal a decision about a request, contact us at support@corporatecreditsolutions.com. Please tell us which request you are making so that we can handle it appropriately.

Before acting on a request, Corporate Credit Solutions LLC may take reasonable steps to verify your identity, and may ask you for additional information for that purpose. We may decline a request where applicable law permits or requires us to do so, and we will respond within any period required by applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right that applies to you.

8. Third-Party Links

Our site contains links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
